# Summary Column Readability Requirement

## Issue

The generated invoice workbook contains per-item total columns for before-tax and after-tax amounts. The grand total values are currently difficult to identify because they appear in the general source-data area instead of being visually associated with those summary columns.

## Requirement

The workbook must make the per-item total columns visually prominent and easy to scan. Grand totals for before-tax and after-tax amounts must be presented in a dedicated summary area aligned horizontally with their corresponding per-item total columns. The summary values and labels must remain clearly distinguishable from source-data rows, and the summary columns should have enough width for their headings and amounts to remain readable.
